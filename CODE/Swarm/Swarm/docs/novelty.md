# Contribution / Novelty Statement

This work proposes an inference-time, decentralized knowledge-transfer framework enabling heterogeneous agents to share compact, model-agnostic "Knowledge Packets" that allow low-capacity peers to skip costly per-frame inference when the swarm collectively agrees a detection is valid. Unlike Federated Learning, which focuses on collaborative model training and weight aggregation over multiple training rounds, our approach operates at inference time: it transfers *predictions and their lightweight embeddings* across agents to make immediate runtime decisions. This yields several distinct advantages: (1) reduced per-frame compute and energy consumption on constrained edge robots, (2) lower communication overhead compared to exchanging raw images or model updates, and (3) compatibility with heterogeneous detectors since embeddings are produced by a shared lightweight encoder rather than combining model weights. The contribution is a practical, deployable protocol and system architecture for energy-aware multi-robot perception that complements (rather than replaces) federated or centralized training methods.
